# FrameOn Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 3, 2026

**Last Updated:** August 18, 2026

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes the limited information processed in connection with the FrameOn application and associated FrameOn-operated services.

FrameOn is designed to perform ordinary media downloading, processing, and conversion operations locally on the user's device.

## 2. Local Media Processing

FrameOn does not intentionally transmit downloaded or converted media, media titles, filenames, download addresses, or search terms to FrameOn-operated services.

FrameOn does not intentionally collect computer usernames, hostnames, MAC addresses, hardware serial numbers, advertising identifiers, precise location information, clipboard contents, directory contents, screen recordings, or information concerning unrelated application activity.

## 3. Browser Cookie Access

Some websites require the user to be signed in before allowing access to certain media. The user may choose an installed browser profile and allow FrameOn's local download tool to use the existing signed-in sessions stored in that profile.

This may include cookies for supported websites such as YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook. Availability depends on the website, the selected browser profile, and support provided by FrameOn's local download tool. Selecting a browser profile does not guarantee that every website or download will work.

Browser cookies can allow a website to recognize the user's existing signed-in session. FrameOn does not request the user's website passwords. Cookie values are processed on the user's device and are not intentionally sent to FrameOn-operated services or included in diagnostic reports. When cookies are enabled, FrameOn may create a private, temporary cookie file so download steps can reuse the approved session without repeatedly requesting browser or Keychain access. This file remains on the user's device and is deleted when FrameOn closes normally.

## 4. Connected Services

FrameOn may communicate with FrameOn-operated services to determine application availability, obtain update information, verify supported requests, retrieve application components, and provide related functionality.

Information received through these connections may include:

- the public IP address associated with the connection;
- the date and time of the request;
- application version and build;
- operating system and system architecture;
- distribution type and update channel;
- user-agent information;
- request and response information; and
- response time.

This information is processed to operate and maintain FrameOn-operated services, diagnose failures, manage service demand, and protect the availability and integrity of those services.

## 5. Security and Operational Records

FrameOn may create and review operational or security records concerning requests made to FrameOn-operated services. Such records may include the information described above together with information indicating whether a request was invalid, rejected, excessive, automated, unsupported, expired, duplicated, or potentially disruptive.

This information may be reviewed, correlated, summarized, or geographically approximated to detect and investigate misuse, identify invalid or disruptive request patterns, enforce reasonable service limitations, diagnose failures, protect service availability, and maintain the integrity and reliability of FrameOn-operated services.

This processing is limited to interactions with FrameOn-operated services. It is not used to monitor media processed locally or unrelated activity on the user's device. FrameOn does not process this information for advertising, sale, or cross-service behavioral profiling. Security and operational monitoring cannot be disabled while communicating with FrameOn-operated services.

## 6. Local Diagnostic Logs

FrameOn keeps a rotating diagnostic log on the user's device. Depending on the activity performed, the local log may include:

- dates and times;
- application actions, update activity, and selected quality, format, or performance settings;
- paths used to locate tools or read and write files;
- download addresses, suggested media titles, output filenames, and output paths;
- the display name of a browser profile selected for a local cookie-assisted download, but not the browser's cookie values;
- processing-tool output and status information; and
- warnings, error messages, exception details, and stack traces.

FrameOn does not intentionally record media-file contents, browser cookie values, passwords, or authentication tokens in its local diagnostic log. Information produced by third-party processing tools may also appear in the log when needed to explain a failure.

The complete local log file is not automatically uploaded. A user may view or save it from FrameOn's Debug window and should review a saved log before sharing it because it may contain download addresses, media titles, filenames, browser-profile names, and filesystem paths.

## 7. Error Reporting

Production versions of FrameOn use Sentry as an error-reporting provider. An automatically submitted error report may include the application version, operating system, runtime, system and process architecture, processor count, operation and failure category, error message, stack trace, relevant processing-tool error output, and filtered recent diagnostic activity.

FrameOn filters web addresses and common user-directory names from FrameOn log messages before sending them. Default personally identifying information is disabled. These safeguards reduce the information included in a report but cannot guarantee that text produced by the operating system or a third-party tool never contains identifying information.

When a user selects Report a Bug, FrameOn sends the user's description, optional name and email address, and the technical context described above to Sentry. The complete local log file is not attached to the report.

Diagnostic information is processed solely to investigate failures, correct defects, and maintain the reliability of FrameOn.

## 8. Log Retention

Operational, security, and diagnostic logs are ordinarily retained for no longer than 90 days.

Relevant records may be retained longer when reasonably necessary to investigate misuse, respond to a security incident, comply with law, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. Aggregated or de-identified information that can no longer reasonably be associated with a particular connection may be retained for a longer period.

## 9. Disclosure and Service Providers

FrameOn does not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed to service providers that perform hosting, infrastructure, security, or error-reporting functions on behalf of FrameOn. Such providers are permitted to process information only for the applicable service purpose and subject to their contractual and legal obligations.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or valid legal process, investigate or respond to misuse or attacks, protect the rights, safety, or property of FrameOn or others, establish or defend legal claims, or complete a lawful transfer of the FrameOn project or its operating assets.

## 10. Information Security

FrameOn applies reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information processed and the risks presented.

No method of transmission, processing, or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 11. Privacy Requests

Privacy requests will be considered in accordance with applicable law. FrameOn does not provide public access to operational or security records.

Information may be withheld, limited, or redacted where disclosure is not legally required or could compromise security, reveal confidential detection methods, expose another person's information, interfere with an investigation, or impair the integrity of FrameOn-operated services.

Because FrameOn does not require user accounts, FrameOn may be unable to identify records associated with a particular person without sufficient, verifiable information.

Requests may be submitted to contact@choatehome.com.

## 12. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be amended to reflect changes to FrameOn, its associated services, or applicable requirements.

Material revisions will be identified by updating the effective date and may be communicated through the application, release notes, or an official FrameOn publication.
